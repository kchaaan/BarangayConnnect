Administrator Guide

Quick guidance for authorized BarangayConnect staff.

Sign in securely

Use your assigned administrator account. Keep your password private and sign out when finished on a shared device.

Review the dashboard

Check summary cards and recent activity, then use the sidebar to open the module you need.

Manage resident accounts

Use User Management to search resident accounts, update contact details, and start password recovery after verifying identity in person.

Process requests and reports

Review the submitted details, add clear remarks, and update each record to reflect the action taken.

Track borrowing and inventory

Review borrowing requests, keep quantities accurate, and record item release and return promptly.

Keep transparency records current

Publish accurate project and budget information. Upload only documents approved for public viewing.

Publish announcements

Check dates, wording, and category before publishing so residents receive useful, accurate updates.

Maintain emergency information

Verify phone numbers and procedures before making them visible to residents.

Protect resident information

Access personal records only for official barangay work. Share the minimum information needed to handle a case.

Admin functions and resident information are available only to staff with the appropriate account permissions.
